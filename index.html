Red Corner Management Information System (RCMIS)

The Problem: Fragmented management of an 18-room hotel and commercial rentals caused unsynchronized online/walk-in reservations, severe double-booking risks, manual notebook-based utility calculations, and administrative blind spots in staff scheduling.

The Solution: A comprehensive architecture integrating a Dual-Database Reservation subsystem, semi-automated tenant billing pipelines, real-time inventory threshold alerts, and an executive support dashboard for centralized decision-making.
