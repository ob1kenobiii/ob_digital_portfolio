RCMIS: Red Corner Management Information System

Engineering a multi-tiered hospitality platform mapping Office Automation (OAS), Management Information (MIS), and Executive Support (ESS) architectures.

Systems Analysis & Design Cross-Subsystem Database Operational Dashboard

The Operational Bottleneck

Red-Corner Hotel and Residences operates an 18-room hotel along with 4 commercial leasing spaces. Management faced massive friction due to operational fragmentation across its mixed-use business model:

  • Information Asymmetry & Double Bookings: Daily reservations from walk-ins and disparate third-party online platforms (Agoda, Airbnb, Facebook) were maintained on unsynchronized logs, leading to frequent double bookings and customer hostility.
  • Utility Billing Overhead: Monthly commercial and residential submeter tracking was recorded entirely in physical notebooks, requiring tedious manual calculations via basic spreadsheet templates across inconsistent tenant start dates.
  • Logistics and Staffing Friction: Room inventory, item restocking requests, and employee bi-weekly scheduling configurations were handled through offline communication channels, creating administrative blind spots.

Proposed Subsystem Integration

The RCMIS engineering model resolves these inefficiencies by linking walk-in and digital online booking protocols directly to a unified database cluster. The system design introduces four primary data pipelines:

Dual-Database Booking Subsystem

Isolates real-time reservation schedules from active room statuses to prevent transactional lockups, immediately reflecting check-in/out updates across channels.

Semi-Automated Tenant Billing

Consolidates water and electricity submeter data inputs to instantly compute weighted consumption totals, taxes, penalties, and lease dues.

Real-Time Inventory Control

Monitors guest amenity usage and cleaning supplies, flagging automated reorder alerts when inventory drops below designated audit thresholds.

Collaborative Staff Scheduler

Generates balanced bi-weekly shift matrices by automatically matching institutional labor needs with individual employee shift preference profiles.

Executive Support & Analytics Dashboard

To serve executive operations requirements, RCMIS abstracts relational data out of secondary subsystem databases into a high-level administrative intelligence layer:

// High-Fidelity Analytic Modeling Views

● Channels Analysis: Visualizes comparative percentages of walk-in versus online revenue distributions.

● Occupancy Optimization: Tracks dynamic daily utilization indicators to predict high-traffic calendar blocks.

● Financial Ledger Aggregation: Pulls real-time transactional aggregates calculating current monthly revenues, daily income flows, and pending balances.

Non-Functional System Compliance

The technical proposal enforces localized data confidentiality standards. Sensitive financial payment tracking fields are designated for field-level encryption, while role-based permission boundaries restrict schedule manipulation and database updates solely to authorized administrative groups.
