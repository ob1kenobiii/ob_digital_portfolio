Commercia: Commercial Space Rental Platform

Designing an interactive B2B SaaS system model tailored to SME real estate sourcing.

High-Fidelity Prototype UI/UX Architecture Systems Analysis

The Concept & Market Gap

Small and medium enterprises (SMEs) face significant overhead friction when searching for verified commercial real estate locations. Information asymmetry, unvetted middle-agents, and complex physical lease negotiation pipelines present barriers to agile business expansion.

System Prototyping & UX Wireframes

Because Commercia is in its design phase, the engineering focus centered on refining user experiences and gathering complete functional specifications before jumping into raw codebase development.

Landlord Interface

Allows listing verifications, spatial dimension specifications, dynamic lease-rate scaling, and localized dashboard insights.

SME Tenant Portal

Includes custom category filtering, interactive structural map overlays, and instant application sub-modules.

Future System Scaling

The UI/UX blueprint sets up a structured path for future engineering sprints. The next stage of development will target integrating third-party map APIs and building out an identity-verification service to guarantee property listing authenticity.

Conceptual Disclaimer

Commercia is an original academic system concept. All property descriptions, financial values, and company portfolios referenced inside this user experience study are mock illustrations used to test platform viability.
